# News Test Set

Usage: start each news item with a second-level Markdown heading (`##`). The heading becomes the result filename. Replace the sample items below with formal test news if needed.

## Sample News 1

A city government announced on Monday that public transit ridership this year increased by 20 percent compared with last year. The report argues that the ridership growth was mainly caused by a newly opened subway line, and therefore road congestion in the city will clearly decline next year.

## Sample News 2

A technology company announced that its new battery can be charged and discharged 3,000 times under laboratory conditions. The report argues that this technology will completely replace current electric-vehicle batteries within two years.
